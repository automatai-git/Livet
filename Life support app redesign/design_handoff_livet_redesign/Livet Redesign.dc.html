Livet · redesign brief

One system for nine apps — and the next ones

Grounded in the real codebase: DM Serif Display + Inter, deep green #1B3B2F, warm off-white, one muted accent per app. What reads "cheap" today: nine full-bleed solid-colour blocks competing at equal volume, box-in-box agenda, redundant "Open X" CTAs, and overflow-prone rows. The fix isn't new colours — it's demoting the accents from paint-bucket fills to small marks (dots, tints, icon colour) on calm ivory surfaces, and giving the home screen a hierarchy: today's actions → most used (auto-sorted from localStorage) → everything else.

1a is the current app, recreated for reference. 1b–1d are three navigation/home models sharing one refined visual language; 1e proves that language on a sub-page (Mobility). My recommendation: 1c's tab-bar shell with 1b's content hierarchy — it scales to Finance and beyond without the hub round-trip.

Figure rules. Every glyph: 24×24, 1.6 stroke, currentColor, round caps — one sprite, mounted once. Icon chips: 10–14px radius, accent at 9–13% opacity behind the accent-coloured stroke.
